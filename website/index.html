How it works

How it works

Voice typing built directly into the system: no extra windows, clipboard hops, or context switching.

Global Hotkey

Press Alt + V in any active application. While held down, Aura records voice input smoothly in the background.

Local Processing

Whisper and Parakeet models process audio directly on your PC — powered by CPU or NVIDIA GPU.

Direct Cursor Typing

Formatted text with automatic punctuation and capitalization appears directly where your cursor is positioned.
